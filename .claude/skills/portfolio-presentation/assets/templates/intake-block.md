## Signals

Every entry records its source and a confidence tier: **verbatim** (transcript timestamp or email text), **recap** (the candidate's own account), or **inference** (drawn from cited entries, not stated in them). Add `ASR-uncertain` only when a machine transcript rates the passage, or a correction to it, below high confidence; high-confidence corrections take no flag. Public context also records a URL and retrieval date. Round facts (date, names, format) come only from this round's sources; leave a missing fact blank and ask. Cite timestamps exactly as the source prints them (turn start or the transcript's own correction table); never estimate one.
